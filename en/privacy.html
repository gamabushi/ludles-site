Privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Who processes your data

The data controller is Mattia Posa. For any question about your data you can write to ludlesapp@gmail.com.

What data we collect

  • Account: email and password (the password is stored encrypted by our provider; we never see it). If you sign in with Discord or Twitch, that service gives us your email, its own account identifier and, if you have them, your profile name and picture: we never see that service’s password.
  • Profile: nickname, photo, bio and the platforms you play on.
  • Gaming IDs, if you add them: your names on PlayStation Network, Xbox, Steam, Nintendo and Discord.
  • Content you publish: posts, images, links, comments, reviews, lobbies, messages in lobby chats and private messages.
  • Played games: the games you add to your profile, with rating, platform, whether you finished or platinumed them and any reviews.
  • Activity: votes, Ludles, blocked users, reports, matches played together, games you want to hear about when a lobby opens, until when you say you are «Available now», lobby invites received and sent, games skipped in Explore and the resulting notifications.
  • Bugs and ideas you send us: the text, the phone’s system (Android, iOS or web) and the app version.
  • Phone notifications: if you turn them on, a code of your device used to send you alerts. We delete it when you sign out; you can choose which alerts to receive from Settings → Phone alerts, or turn them off in your phone settings.
  • Ludles+ subscription: only whether it is active and until when. Payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store: we never see your card details.
  • Technical data: IP address and service logs.
  • Advertising: the device’s advertising identifiers, used by Google AdMob for personalized ads only if you give consent.
  • Language: the language you use the app in, to send you alerts in the same language.

Why we use it

  • To let you use Ludles: account, lobbies, chat, posts and connections with other players (performance of the contract).
  • For safety and moderation: handling reports and blocks, preventing abuse (legitimate interest).
  • To fix problems and improve the app with the reports and ideas you send us (legitimate interest).
  • For personalized advertising, only with your consent, which you can change at any time from Settings → Ad consent.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Who else sees the data

Nickname, photo, bio, posts, comments, lobbies and Ludles are visible to the other users of the app. Others can also see your played games, unless you make them private from your profile: when they are visible, the games you finish, platinum or review also appear in the posts feed (you can delete these posts), and your ratings are used to suggest players with similar taste. Your gaming IDs are seen by your Ludles and by people in a lobby with you, or by everyone if you choose so. If you say you are «Available now», until it expires (at most 4 hours) others see it on your profile and, if the game is among your visible played games, so do the members of lobbies for that game, who can invite you: you can turn invites off from Settings → Privacy. Your actions in the app (e.g. lobbies opened, matches played, reviews, comments, likes) earn experience points: we keep the list of points earned, which only you can see, while your level is visible to everyone on your profile. Messages in lobby chats and the Discord voice channel link are seen only by the lobby members; private messages only by you and the recipient. To run the app we rely on providers that process data on our behalf: Supabase (database and storage), Discord and Twitch (only if you use them to sign in), Google AdMob (advertising), RevenueCat (subscription status), Google Play and Apple App Store (payments), Expo with Google Firebase and Apple (delivery of phone notifications: they receive the alert text and the device code), Sentry (app error reporting: it receives the type of error, the phone model, the app version and your internal identifier, not your email or nickname). We do not sell your data.

Transfers outside the European Union

Some providers are based in the United States. In these cases the transfer is protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

How long

We keep the data as long as your account exists. You can delete it whenever you want from Settings → Delete account (if you can no longer sign in to the app, follow the instructions at https://gamabushi.github.io/ludles-site/en/delete-account.html): profile, content and Ludles are deleted immediately; the provider’s backup copies disappear within a few days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to its use or withdraw consent, by writing to ludlesapp@gmail.com. If you believe your data is being processed improperly you can file a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it).

Minimum age

Ludles is reserved for people aged 14 or over.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the date at the top and, for important changes, we tell you in the app.
