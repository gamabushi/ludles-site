Account deletion

You can delete your Ludles account and all your data at any time.

From the app (the fastest way)

  1. Open Ludles and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account and security → Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm and tap “Delete forever”.

If you can no longer sign in to the app

Write to ludlesapp@gmail.com from your account’s email address (or telling us your nickname), with the subject “Account deletion”. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it’s done.

What gets deleted

  • your profile, photo, nickname and gaming IDs;
  • your posts, comments, votes, reviews and played games;
  • your Ludles and messages;
  • your presence in lobbies (if you were the host, the role passes to another member).

We keep nothing after deletion: our provider’s (Supabase) backup copies disappear within a few days. If you have Ludles+, cancel the subscription on Google Play first: deleting the account doesn’t stop renewals.

Contact: ludlesapp@gmail.com
