Child safety standards

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Zero tolerance

Any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation is forbidden on Ludles. In particular:

  • child sexual abuse material (photos, videos, drawings or texts that sexualize minors);
  • grooming of minors, including in private messages or lobby chats;
  • sexual blackmail (sextortion) and requests for intimate images;
  • any content or behavior that puts a minor at risk.

Minimum age

Ludles is only for people aged 14 or older. Accounts of younger people are closed as soon as we find out.

How to report

In the app you can report profiles, posts, comments, reviews, lobbies, lobby messages and private messages with the “Report” button, and block anyone. For anything about a child’s safety you can also write to ludlesapp@gmail.com, with the subject “Child safety”. If a child is in immediate danger, call the emergency number right away (112 in Europe).

What we do

Reports about child safety take priority over all others. When we find content or behavior of this kind:

  • we remove it immediately;
  • we permanently close the account that published it;
  • we preserve what investigators need and report it to the competent authorities, in Italy the Polizia Postale (the national center against online child sexual abuse), as the law requires.

How we prevent risks

  • Private messages are only possible between Ludles, people who have both accepted the connection.
  • Content reported by several people disappears for everyone, even before we review it.
  • A reminder of the rules is always shown when uploading photos and videos.
  • The terms of use forbid sexual content and provide for closing the account.

Contact

The child safety contact is the owner of Ludles, who answers at ludlesapp@gmail.com. Ludles’ public pages are at https://gamabushi.github.io/ludles-site/.
