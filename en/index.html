Ludles

Find teammates to play online co-op with.

Coming soon to Google Play: it’s in beta right now.

Ludles is a free Android app: open a lobby for the game you want, find who’s up for playing right now and get organized in the chat. Around it there’s video game news, the players’ feed and your profile with the games you’ve played.
